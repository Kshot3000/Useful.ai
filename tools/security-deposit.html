After move-out

Security deposit timeline

Enter the move-out date and the deadline in days from your state's statute or the lease, whichever you confirmed. This page does not look up or invent that number. It dates the demand and keeps a photo log.

  1. Write down the date you returned keys and possession. That is the date this timeline starts from, unless your lease or statute uses a different trigger. Confirm which one applies.
  2. Look up the return deadline on the state legislature or attorney-general site, and read the lease. Type the number of days below. Do not guess.
  3. Photograph each room, appliance, and any damage the landlord listed. Log them here with the date taken.
  4. Send a forwarding address in writing if the statute or lease requires it. This page does not know whether yours does.
  5. If the deadline passes with no itemized statement or refund, copy the demand letter and send it the way the statute requires (mail, certified, or otherwise). Confirm that method yourself.
Fill the fields, then build the packet.

Not legal, tax, or medical advice. Return deadlines, interest, penalties, and how you must send notice are state law and lease language. Confirm them on an official source before you rely on the date this page calculates.
