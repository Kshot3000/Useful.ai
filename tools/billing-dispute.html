Billing dispute packet

Type the amount on the statement and the amount you agreed to. This page subtracts them, keeps a log of what you can attach, and writes the letter. The dispute window stays blank until you enter the days you confirmed on the notice or the issuer’s page. It does not state a statute.

  1. 01
    Copy the charge from the statement, not from memory. The merchant name and the posted amount are what the issuer will look up.
  2. 02
    Type only the amount you authorized, or the receipt total. A blank authorized amount is not treated as zero.
  3. 03
    Look up the dispute window on the statement, the card agreement, or the issuer’s page. Leave the days empty if you have not confirmed them.
