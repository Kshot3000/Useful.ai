Out-of-pocket max tracker

Type the deductible, the percent you pay after it, and the out-of-pocket max from your plan summary. Then add claims from the EOBs. The page runs that arithmetic. It does not look up a plan or a price.

  1. 01
    Copy the deductible, coinsurance, and out-of-pocket max from the summary of benefits. Individual and family figures are often different — track the one the portal is using.
  2. 02
    If the portal already shows progress, type those amounts. Leave them blank only if this year has not been applied yet.
  3. 03
    Add each claim’s allowed amount from the EOB, in date order. A fixed copay is a number you type, not a rate this page knows.

Model on this page: until the deductible you entered is met, you pay the allowed amount. After that, you pay the percent you entered of what is left. That share stops at the out-of-pocket max you entered. Copays, excluded services, out-of-network caps, and family cross-accumulation are not assumed — a fixed amount is only used if you type it. This is not a benefits quote.
