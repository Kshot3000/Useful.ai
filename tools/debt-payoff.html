Avalanche and snowball

Debt payoff order

Enter the balances, APRs, and minimums from the statements. The page orders them two ways and runs the same monthly arithmetic on both. It does not look up a rate.

  1. 01
    Type each balance, APR, and minimum as printed. This page does not fetch a bureau or a card issuer.
  2. 02
    Add any extra you can send after every minimum. Leave it blank if there is none.
  3. 03
    Avalanche puts the extra on the highest APR. Snowball puts it on the smallest balance. A freed minimum rolls to the next debt in that order.

Each month the page adds balance × APR ÷ 12, then applies that debt’s minimum, then the extra, to the target. Fees, promo end dates, and daily compounding are not in the model. If a minimum does not cover that month’s interest, the line is flagged — the page does not invent a required payment.
