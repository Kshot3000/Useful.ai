Same scope, three bids

Contractor bid comparison

Put the work you actually want priced on one list, then type what each bid charged for that line. A blank stays a missing item. This page does not look up license status, typical prices, or how long a quote is good for.

  1. 01
    Name the job and the three contractors. A bid that is not in hand stays blank.
  2. 02
    Write the scope once. Every bid is scored against that list, not against its own headings.
  3. 03
    Enter the price each bid gave for that line. Blank means missing. Extras sit outside the shared scope.

Quote windows, deposit rules, and license requirements are on the proposal or your state board. This page will not fill them in.

Leave a cell blank if that bid did not price the line. Do not guess a number to make the columns match.

A line that only one bid named. It counts in that bid’s line sum, and it is flagged as not on the shared list.
