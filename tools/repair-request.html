Landlord repair request packet

Type what is broken, when you noticed it, and when someone can come in. This page writes the request and counts a follow-up date only from the days you enter. It does not state a repair statute, a habitability rule, or a response deadline.

  1. 01
    Name the room and what fails. A photo of the leak is more useful than a legal label you did not look up.
  2. 02
    Give an access window you can actually keep. The letter uses only the hours you type.
  3. 03
    If your lease or the landlord’s notice names a reply period, type those days. Leave them blank if you have not confirmed a number.
