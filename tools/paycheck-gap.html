Stub versus the hours

Paycheck gap packet

The rate, the hours, and the overtime multiplier are yours to type from the offer, the handbook, or the statute you checked. This page only multiplies those numbers, subtracts the gross on the stub, and writes the demand. It does not know a minimum wage.

  1. 01
    Copy hours from your own record, not from memory of the stub. The stub is what you are checking.
  2. 02
    Type the regular rate and the overtime multiplier you confirmed. Leave the multiplier blank if you have not confirmed it. This page will not fill in 1.5 for you.
  3. 03
    Compare gross to gross. Taxes and other deductions stay in the notes. A net-pay gap is not the same claim.
