Move-in condition report

Walk the unit and type what you see. This page turns room notes, meter readings you read off the dials, and a photo log into a dated report you can send with the lease. It does not state a habitability rule, a deposit statute, or a deadline.

  1. 01
    Walk every room before you unpack. A stain you do not log becomes your problem at move-out.
  2. 02
    Read the meters yourself. This page does not look up a utility rate or a starting balance.
  3. 03
    Send the packet the way the lease says, and keep the copy. Days until the lease starts are arithmetic on the dates you type.
