Explanation of benefits

Medical EOB decoder

Paste the labels the notice already uses: billed, allowed, plan paid, patient responsibility. The page reads those words in plain English and checks whether the columns add. It does not look up a code or invent a price.

  1. 01
    Use the explanation of benefits, not the provider’s first statement. The column names vary. Copy the words on your notice.
  2. 02
    Paste labeled amounts, or type service lines as description | billed | allowed | plan paid | you owe. Leave a column blank if the notice does not show it.
  3. 03
    Appeal windows and remark-code meanings are printed on the notice. This page does not invent them. Copy the packet before you call.

Not legal, tax, or medical advice. Dated 3 October 2026. Every dollar on this page is one you typed. A billed amount above allowed is common and is not, by itself, an error. No CPT or HCPCS lookup.
