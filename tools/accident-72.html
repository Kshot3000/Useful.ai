Before the claim gets messy

72 hours after a crash

A sequence, then a packet you can copy. Deadlines differ by state and policy. This page does not invent them. If anyone is hurt, call emergency services first.

  1. Move out of traffic if the cars can move and it is safe. Turn on hazards.
  2. Call emergency services if anyone is injured, a car cannot move, or the other driver leaves.
  3. Photograph plates, all four corners, the scene, skid marks, and the other driver's license and insurance card.
  4. Get the other driver's name, insurer, policy number, and the reporting officer's name and report number.
  5. Do not give a recorded statement to the other insurer until you have your own claim open.
  6. Tell your insurer. Ask how long you have to report a claim. Write that date down. This page does not know it.
  7. See a clinician if you hurt, even if it feels minor. Keep discharge papers.
  8. Get one written repair estimate before authorizing tear-down. Keep the rental policy limit in writing.

Not legal advice. Reporting duties and suit deadlines are state law and policy language. Confirm both with your insurer and, if the damage or injury is serious, a lawyer in that state.
