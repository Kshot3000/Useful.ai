Bill and explanation of benefits

Medical bill dispute packet

The chargemaster number is usually not what the plan allowed. This page separates those two, flags lines you pasted twice, and writes the letter that asks for the itemized bill before anyone talks about collections.

  1. 01
    Use the explanation of benefits, not the first statement. Allowed, paid, and patient responsibility are on the EOB.
  2. 02
    Ask the provider for an itemized bill if the statement is one lump sum. Paste each line below.
  3. 03
    Send the letter to the provider’s billing office and a copy to the insurer’s appeals address on the EOB. The appeal window is printed there. This page does not invent it.

Not legal or medical advice. A billed amount above the allowed amount is normal. A patient balance above allowed minus paid is the figure to ask about. It is not proof of an error.
