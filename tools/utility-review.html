Utility bill review packet

Type the months, the amounts charged, and the usage printed on the bills. This page averages those figures and writes a review letter. It does not look up a tariff, a kilowatt-hour price, or a dispute deadline.

  1. 01
    Copy the account number and the service address off the bill. Leave a field blank if you have not confirmed it.
  2. 02
    Log each month: the period, the amount charged, and the usage units printed on that bill. This page does not estimate a rate.
  3. 03
    If a reply period is printed on the bill or the account page you opened, type those days. Leave them blank if you have not confirmed one.
