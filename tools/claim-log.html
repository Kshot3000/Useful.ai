Insurance claim log

Type the claim number, what you asked to be paid, and what has been paid so far. This page subtracts those figures and writes a status letter. It does not state a payment deadline, a coverage rule, or a statute.

  1. 01
    Copy the claim number and the amounts off the notice or the portal. Do not estimate a payment this page did not see.
  2. 02
    Log each call, email, and estimate. A dated line is more useful than a memory of who said what.
  3. 03
    If the letter or the policy names a reply period, type those days. Leave them blank if you have not confirmed a number.
