Unpaid invoice packet

Type the invoice lines and what has already been received. This page subtracts those figures and writes a follow-up letter. It does not look up a payment term, a late fee, or a collections deadline.

  1. 01
    Copy the invoice number, the issue date, and the due date off the invoice you sent. Leave a date blank if you have not confirmed it.
  2. 02
    Log each line and the amount received. The open balance is those figures subtracted. This page does not estimate a fee.
  3. 03
    If a reply window or a late fee is printed on the contract or the invoice you sent, type it. Leave it blank if you have not confirmed one.
