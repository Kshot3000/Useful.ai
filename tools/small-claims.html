Before the filing window

Small-claims worksheet

Add the amounts you are asking for, subtract what you already received, and index the exhibits. The filing fee and the dollar limit stay blank until you type the figures from the court that will hear it. This page does not list courts or addresses.

  1. 01
    Name the court the way its own site does. Do not guess the building. Filing place, fee, and limit are on that court's page or clerk form.
  2. 02
    Type the principal and any other out-of-pocket you can point to. Interest is only what you enter, or simple interest on a rate you type. This is not a statutory rate.
  3. 03
    Index each exhibit before you file. Whether the fee can be added to the judgment is a box you check only after the form says so.

Simple interest, if you choose it, is principal × rate × days ÷ 365. That day count is a convention on this page, not a court rule.

Example fills a sample dispute only. The fee, the service cost, and the dollar limit stay blank so a sample does not look like a court schedule.
